# Terms of service

Last updated: 2026-09-26

These terms cover the paid option in Slop Filter, "Slop Filter's key". The extension itself is open source software under the MIT license; you run it yourself and these terms do not apply to it.

## What you get

Scoring of posts with the Jev model through our server, on our key, for as long as your subscription is active. Scoring is best effort: it can be wrong, it can be slow, and it can be down. We do not promise uptime.

## Price and billing

- $3.99 a month, billed by Stripe. The subscription renews every month until you cancel.
- The free trial is 100 scored posts, with no time limit. A card is required to start it. When the 100th post is scored, the subscription starts and the card is charged.
- The trial is given once per card and once per email. A card or email that already had one is charged when the subscription is set up.
- If a renewal fails, scoring keeps working for 7 days while Stripe retries the card, then stops until it is paid.
- We may change the price. We will email you at least 30 days before a change applies to you.

## Canceling

Cancel anytime from the account page ("Manage billing"). Canceling takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for; scoring keeps working until then. Deleting your account cancels right away.

## Refunds

None. Not for unused time, not for unused posts, not for change of mind. Where the law gives you a right to a refund, that right stands.

## Limits

- 3,000 scored posts a day and 30,000 a month per account. These are fuses against runaway use, not a promise.
- One account per person. The service is for the extension, not for scripts, bots, or reselling. We may suspend an account that abuses it.

## Your data

See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md). Stripe holds your card. We hold your email, your account state, and daily counts, never the posts.

We post changes here with a new date. Keeping the subscription after a change means you accept it.
